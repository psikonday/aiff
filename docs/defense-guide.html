Questions and answers

Why is BFS appropriate?

All legal moves have equal cost. BFS expands cells by distance and therefore finds a shortest path. For unequal costs, use UCS or Dijkstra instead.

Is a clear sensor reading proof of a free space?

No. With the default assumptions, the occupied probability after no alert is 1/13, or about 7.7%.

Why does an alert produce 75%?

The occupied-and-alert probability is 0.9 x 0.4 = 0.36. Total alert probability is 0.36 + 0.2 x 0.6 = 0.48. The posterior is 0.36/0.48 = 0.75.

What are the MDP states?

Candidate index, occupancy belief rounded to 1%, and remaining recheck budget. There are 3 x 101 x 2 = 606 nonterminal states.

What makes this more than a one-step score?

Value Iteration includes the discounted value of the next state. Rechecking branches into two possible observations, and skipping or failing can lead to another candidate.

Is it reinforcement learning?

It is model-based planning with known transition and reward assumptions. It does not learn a Q-function from experience and is not Q-learning.

Is this an exact solution to partially observed parking?

No. Hidden occupancy motivates a belief state. The implementation discretizes beliefs and uses a simplified candidate-order model, so it is an approximate belief-state MDP.

Why can all alerts lead to Check instead of no space?

An imperfect alert does not prove occupancy. If the expected benefit of another reading exceeds its cost and the alternatives, the policy chooses Check. It draws no driving route until driving is allowed.

Why is the route sometimes different from the illustration?

The illustration is one example. The interactive route uses the current map and beliefs. Several paths can share the same shortest length.

Did you use AI to create this?

Yes. ChatGPT substantially assisted with implementation, tests, debugging and documentation. The declaration describes its role. I can explain the parts I reviewed and demonstrate their behavior.

What are the main limitations?

Synthetic readings, assumed sensor rates, a small grid, discretized beliefs, fixed candidate order, and a simplified model of travel after a failed parking attempt.
