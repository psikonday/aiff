Mini Smart Parking Guide

A parking decision under uncertainty

Dilnaz, Aruana, Madina and Alnur AI Fundamentals final project

Speaker notesProblem statement and objectives: begin the two-minute introduction. Explain that the application is a simulation.

The parking problem

A nearby space may be occupied. A short route may be blocked.

Objective: combine noisy sensor readings with a legal route and a reasoned next action.

Speaker notesExplain the practical problem without claiming measured savings. State that sensors and rewards are assumptions.

Three course topics

BFS: shortest path on an unweighted grid Bayesian inference: occupancy from a noisy reading Value Iteration: action choice over future outcomes

Course alignment: Week 2, Week 6 and Week 5

Speaker notesSpend about two minutes on algorithms across this slide and the next. These are distinct course topics.

A decision you can inspect

40% prior becomes 75% after an alert. No alert gives 7.7% occupancy. The MDP can Drive, Check, Skip or Exit.

606 planning states. Discount 0.95. One recheck per candidate. Beliefs rounded to 1%.

Speaker notesExplain Bayes: 0.36/0.48=0.75. Explain Bellman backups, transition probabilities and why this is model-based planning rather than learned Q-learning.

Live demonstration

Original: B in 6 steps All clear: A in 4 steps Only C clear: C in 8 steps All alerts: recheck before driving

Then block the exit, remove an obstacle and export a scenario.

Speaker notesUse the live application for three to five minutes. Do not promise that every alert means occupancy. Show calculation values and recheck controls.

Tests and evidence

20 automated tests passed 152 prior and signal combinations 300 shortest-path comparisons on 100 maps

Analytical Bayes checks, Bellman consistency, route safety and recheck outcomes.

Speaker notesRun node tests/run.js. The map comparison uses an independent repeated-relaxation oracle and seed 12345. Synthetic tests do not establish real-world sensor accuracy.

Scope and responsibility

Synthetic sensor readings Approximate belief-state planning Shared team review with declared AI assistance

Live demo: psikonday.github.io/aiff/ Source: github.com/psikonday/aiff

Speaker notesLimitations include conditional independence, fixed candidate order and distances measured from the entrance. All members should be ready for individual questions. ChatGPT substantially assisted the project.
