# AI Usage Declaration

**Project:** Mini Smart Parking Guide  
**Course:** AI Fundamentals  
**Team:** Dilnaz, Aruana, Madina and Alnur

The Mini Smart Parking Guide was developed by the student team. The team was responsible for the project direction and scope, the selection and integration of the three course topics - Breadth-First Search (BFS), Bayesian inference, and a finite belief-state Markov Decision Process (MDP) solved with Value Iteration - as well as the simulation scenarios, review of results, final decisions and preparation for the project defense.

ChatGPT was used as a supporting tool during the work. It helped the team refine parts of the browser implementation, identify and correct imperfections, debug routing, and generate or improve automated tests. It also helped polish wording and structure for the written report, presentation, demonstration materials and defense preparation. The team reviewed the suggestions and generated material and made the final decisions about what was included in the submitted project.

The project does not claim model training, live sensor measurements or an original field study. The team remains responsible for the submitted application and documents, including checking calculations, verifying outputs and explaining the work individually. ChatGPT support does not replace the team's contribution, understanding or responsibility for the final submission.
