Under the hood
Three ideas, one decision.
The guide is a simple model, but each part has a distinct job.
01
Find the shortest path
Breadth-first search explores the grid one step at a time, avoiding obstacles and likely occupied spaces until it reaches the chosen space.
02
Update the odds
A Bayesian calculation combines a 40% starting chance of occupancy with the sensor reading. An alert raises that estimate to 75%.
03
Make the call
A finite belief-state MDP compares four actions using transition probabilities, rewards and a discount factor. Value Iteration accounts for future outcomes: parking successfully, failing, checking again, skipping a space or leaving.


A note on the model
Simple by design.
Useful to explore.
This is a classroom-sized simulation, not a live parking service. The map, sensor readings and rewards are examples, chosen to show how a system can make a decision when the information is uncertain.
In the original scenario, space A has a 75% occupancy estimate. The planner skips it and selects B, reached in six steps. If all spaces send alerts, another reading may be worth taking before leaving.
Try it yourself: switch space A to “No alert” and see the route change.
